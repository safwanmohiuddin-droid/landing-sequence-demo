# Design context

Established direction: warm Marble surfaces, Gulf blue controls, clay critical path and gold reserved for the keystone. Night switches the whole workspace through CSS variables. Instrument Serif is display; Geist is Latin UI; IBM Plex Sans Arabic is Arabic UI. Monospaced measurements use system fallbacks. One-pixel rules, compact rectangular controls and quiet motion suit an engineering plan rather than a generic dashboard.

Workspace mode: Operate. Desktop uses a broad schedule column and 320px right readout. Mobile orders go-live, timeline/decisions/obligations, keystone, what-if and day-zero tasks. Timeline overflow belongs to its own scroller. Its engineering axis stays LTR, even when the surrounding Arabic UI mirrors. Inspector geometry is stable so blur cannot move a switch under the pointer.

Pitch mode: Persuade. Editorial left-aligned serif headline, sourced numbers, dependency-loop diagram, a sticky checklist-to-graph discovery, and a before/after timeline. Preserve supplied narrative with explicit corrections for uncollected field data. No decoration images or copied brand assets.

Motion uses explicit cubic-bezier easing. Schedule bars and go-live marker animate transforms; the tour spotlight is the signature workspace moment. Reduced-motion shows final states. Manual details use a native modal dialog; guided/autoplay drawers are non-modal so their controls stay reachable. All native controls retain visible focus.

Tokens are in src/index.css and src/pitch/pitch.css. The built-in Impeccable launcher/detector is unavailable because its binary is not installed; manual browser evidence and independent reviews cover the finishing pass.
