# Product context

Landing Sequence is a hackathon planning demo for a COO or head of people relocating a company and its staff to Abu Dhabi. The public pitch explains the sequencing problem. The workspace shows company, people, families and obligations in one sourced dependency graph. Success is a visible operational date and a concrete first step, then an understandable date change when bank readiness or dependencies change.

Frozen Plan/ScheduledPlan and zod contracts remain the integration boundary. Durations come from supplied data, never from the language model. Explain, Draft, Extract and What-if are server features with validated mock fallbacks. Local preview is fully functional without a key. Survey, leasing and school-call field data remain pending; no evidence is fabricated. User requested local inspection first; no publishing or submission.
